        <div class="creators">
            <h3>Who is the Creator</h3>
            <p>Prentis Rollins is a veteran of the American comics industry, having contributed art to such best-selling titles as Green Lantern: Rebirth, Green Lantern Corps, DC: One Million, JLA Incarnations, DC 2000, Power Company, Flash: Iron Heights, Batman: The Ultimate Evil, Hardware, New X-Men, and many others.<br><br>
            He is the author of The Furnace (A Graphic Novel) (Tor Books, 2018), How to Draw Sci-fi Heroes and Villains (Phaidon, 2023), How to Draw Sci-fi Utopias and Dystopias (Phaidon, 2016), The Making of a Graphic Novel (The Resonator) (Penguin/Random House, 2006), and Survival Machine (Stories) (Monkeysuit Press, 2002).<br><br>
            He was featured artist for Volume 3 of the acclaimed graphic novel series Jekyll Island Chronicles (IDW/Top Shelf, 2021). He was co-founder and editor of Monkeysuit Press, which published five critically-acclaimed anthologies of New York-based indie comics creators between 2000-2005. He lives with his family in London.</p>
